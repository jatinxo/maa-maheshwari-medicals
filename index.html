About the store

Trusted medical store
in Lucknow.

Maa Maheshwari Medicals has been a fixture of Bangla Bazar since 2005 — long enough to fill prescriptions for grandparents and now their grandchildren. We're a full-service pharmacy: prescription medicines, everyday health products, and quick in-store health checks, backed by staff who take the time to explain dosage and usage clearly.

Whether it's a monthly refill, a sudden fever at 10 PM, or picking up baby-care essentials on the way home, our aim is the same — genuine products, correct storage, and honest, clear billing every time.
